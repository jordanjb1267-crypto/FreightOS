## The three FAILs, and why they are FAILs rather than gaps

Each is a case where the shipped artifacts **contradict** a requirement, provable without any
runtime:

- **GR-09** — the graphs declare _"duplicate delivery must not duplicate a binding side effect"_ in
  all 36 files, and 10 of their own side-effecting nodes cannot satisfy it.
- **GR-12** — the graphs declare `HOLD` terminal in all 36 files and define it in none, while
  routing 91% of nodes there on failure.
- **GR-15** — an accepted, tested v1.3 control (kill switch) that the package ignores entirely
  across 36 graphs, including nodes that command physical operations and write to customer systems.

All three are closable by editing the shipped graph artifacts — no runtime is required to fix them,
and fixing them before implementation is far cheaper than after.
