# Domain Glossary

- **Shipment:** Commercial requirement to move goods.
- **Consignment:** Goods transported/documented together.
- **Journey:** End-to-end planned/actual movement.
- **Transport Leg:** One modal segment.
- **Load Opportunity:** Uncommitted freight under evaluation.
- **Tender:** Formal offer/request to perform transportation.
- **Booking:** Reserved transport capacity.
- **Powered Unit:** Road vehicle providing motive power.
- **Transport Equipment:** Trailer, chassis, container, railcar, ULD, etc.
- **Carrier-Agent Mode:** Acting for a specified appointed carrier.
- **Brokerage Mode:** Acting through the authorized brokerage entity.
- **Autonomous Operation:** Consequential A4 action under policy.
- **Policy Decision:** Allow, deny, or approval-required.
- **Approval:** Time-bound authorization tied to material parameters.
- **Cell:** Regional or dedicated deployment unit.

## Physical logistics and autonomous mobility

**FacilityOS:** FreightOS product layer coordinating facility readiness, appointments, gate, yard, dock, custody, and receiving.

**Dynamic Driving Task (DDT):** Real-time operational and tactical driving functions. FreightOS does not perform or command the DDT.

**Operational Design Domain (ODD):** Conditions under which an ADS is designed to function. The ADS provider is authoritative for its ODD and mission eligibility.

**Vehicle Visit:** One vehicle arrival, facility service workflow, and departure.

**Custody Transfer:** Evidence-backed transition of cargo responsibility between authorized parties.

**Remote Assistance:** Provider or operator support that may include monitoring, confirmation, guidance, or authorization. FreightOS remote assistance excludes remote driving.

**Facility Digital Twin:** Versioned operational representation of a facility using authoritative geometry, locations, capabilities, restrictions, schedules, and current state.
