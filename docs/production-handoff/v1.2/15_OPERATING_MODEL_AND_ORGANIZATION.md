# Operating Model and Organization

## Initial one-person operation

The owner can direct product, approve policy, and supervise limited production, but production is always-on cloud infrastructure.

Initial responsibilities may be combined but remain explicit:

- Product owner
- Principal engineer
- Security owner
- Carrier operations owner
- Compliance owner
- Incident commander
- Billing owner

## Required separation at scale

Engineering, SRE, security, AI evaluation, carrier operations, brokerage operations, compliance/legal, customer success, finance/billing, and data governance.

## Human supervision

Supervisors receive approvals, low-confidence cases, policy exceptions, high exposure, contract changes, fraud/identity flags, claims, disputes, and incidents.

## Support

Standard, Priority, Mission Critical, Dedicated Cell, Private Deployment. Commitments must be evidence-based.

## Physical operations organization

As the platform expands, establish explicit owners for FacilityOS operations, warehouse integrations, autonomous-mobility safety, provider partnerships, remote-assistance coordination, fleet maintenance, facility certification, and physical-logistics incidents.

No single generalist agent substitutes for a safety owner, incident commander, provider authority, facility operator, or transportation counsel.
