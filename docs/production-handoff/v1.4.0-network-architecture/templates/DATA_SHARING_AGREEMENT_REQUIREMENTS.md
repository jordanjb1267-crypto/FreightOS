# Data-Sharing Agreement Requirements

This is an engineering requirements template, not legal advice.

Address:

- parties and represented organizations;
- purpose and permitted uses;
- data categories and classifications;
- controller/custodian roles;
- security requirements;
- subprocessors and downstream sharing;
- model training and analytics use;
- retention, deletion, and legal holds;
- data residency and transfers;
- audit and evidence;
- incident notification;
- correction and dispute;
- service levels and support;
- termination and access revocation;
- regulatory/document-title boundaries;
- governing law and dispute forum for counsel to complete.
