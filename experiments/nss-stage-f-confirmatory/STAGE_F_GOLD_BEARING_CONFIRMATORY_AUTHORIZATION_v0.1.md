## 5. Semantic gold semantics

Stage-F QuestionSet preserves typed primitive separation.

### Noul

Gold is a frozen binary/event-state truth label for the proposition being queried. Provider probability remains a probabilistic prediction against that truth.

This permits pre-registered Brier score and log-loss without pretending the gold itself is a model probability.

### Choice

Gold is the exact expected option from a frozen finite option set.

### Score

Gold is a deterministic target score derived from the synthetic event/state construction before any provider call, together with any frozen acceptance tolerance/band.
